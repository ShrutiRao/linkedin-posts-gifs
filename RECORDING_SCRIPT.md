# Two Minute Showcase Recording Script

Record this after replacing the synthetic demo with your own approved corpus and final pair. Keep the browser zoom at 100% and hide notifications.

## 0:00–0:20 — What the tool proves

- Open the Start page.
- Say: “This is a privacy-first studio that learns observable patterns from writing I authored, but keeps the raw corpus out of model requests.”
- Point to the five workflow stages and privacy boundary.

## 0:20–0:45 — Audit the corpus

- Upload your normalized CSV.
- Show the valid-post count, missing fields, and metric comparability warning.
- Say: “Performance is context, not proof that a writing pattern caused engagement.”

## 0:45–1:10 — Inspect and correct the voice

- Open two source-backed observations and their post IDs.
- Edit one voice rule that initially sounds too generic.
- Approve the profile.

## 1:10–1:35 — Draft and approve

- Enter the topic, audience, facts, source URL, and one real experience.
- Generate three hooks, select one, and show the supported claim.
- Approve the claim and post.

## 1:35–1:55 — Storyboard and render

- Generate the storyboard and show frame words, visual change, and timing.
- Approve it, render the GIF, and show the static fallback and alt text.

## 1:55–2:20 — Review and export

- Complete the pair checklist.
- Export the sanitized showcase.
- Open the artifact folder and show `post.txt`, `storyboard.md`, `infographic.gif`, `fallback.png`, `alt-text.txt`, and `manifest.json`.
- Finish: “Nothing was posted automatically. Every claim and visual crossed an explicit approval gate.”
